About & Now · The Writer

Hello, I'm Kiên.

Software Artisan & Essayist · Hanoi, Vietnam

I build calm distributed systems by day and write slow essays about Hanoi, tea, and old books by lamplight.

I have spent the last eight years designing event pipelines, payment ledgers, and the unglamorous plumbing that keeps software honest under load.

This journal keeps both halves of my life on the same desk: Side A for systems and code, Side B for the rain, the tea, and the people I meet in the margins of books.
